# Torque question pools

The 20-question test uses 1,353 fine-thread chart exercises. Selection picks a diameter, a compatible chart group, an individual bolt designation, and one nut from that group's tension/shear lists. Combinations do not repeat within an attempt; choices are shuffled by the shared practice engine. The torque answer always comes from the supplied chart, not from the manufacturer catalogs.

All chart headings are parsed into three bolt pools and six nut columns. Generation uses the bolt-size mappings that have been established: AN3–AN20 and their chart-listed DD versions; verified MS20033–20045 sizes; MS20004–20020 sizes; NAS144–158, NAS333–340, NAS583–590, NAS624–640, and NAS1303–1320. Only sizes with matching chart rows are emitted. The range endpoints do not imply that every intervening part number is manufactured.

Manufacturer diameter/thread references:

- [NAS144–158](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/NAS144%20thru%20NAS158.pdf)
- [NAS333–340](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/NAS333%20thru%20NAS340.pdf)
- [NAS583–590](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/NAS583%20thru%20NAS590.pdf)
- [NAS624–644](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/NAS624%20thru%20NAS644.pdf)
- [NAS1303–1320](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/NAS1303%20thru%20NAS1320.pdf)
- [MS20004–20024](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/MS20004%20thru%20MS20024.pdf)
- [MS20033–20046](https://www.fastenerdimensions.com/upload/documentgroups/mainfiles/MS20033%20thru%20MS20046.pdf)

Specific exclusions: NAS835 is absent from the supplied chart. MS20012's listed 3/4-14 thread differs from the chart's 3/4-16 row; NAS1316 and MS20045 have listed 1-12 threads, while the chart's one-inch row is 1-14. Other chart bolt families remain classified in the headings but are not generated until their individual diameter mappings are sourced. No eligible mapped bolt currently uses the #8 row. Coarse-thread questions are outside this test's requested fine-thread scope.

Questions use base bolt and nut designations, as the course exercise does; grip, length, and nut-size suffixes are not part of this lookup exercise. The original 50-question pool and prior 104-combination scores remain separately available as previous-score records. The expanded pool uses a separate saved-progress key to preserve them.

Every expanded question is included in the bank-backed searchable notes. The coverage test checks its correct answer and explanation. Search text is cached, and question-reference accordions render their contents on demand so the larger pool does not slow the initial notes view.
