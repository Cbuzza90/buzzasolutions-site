## Source corrections and omissions

- Presentation 5 matching question 1 prints CC. Later source questions and the course deck use C for corrosion-resistant steel. The unverified CC row is excluded; the confirmed C question is included.
- NAS questions call their dash-field dimension length. The existing course deck describes unthreaded grip in sixteenths. Practice questions explicitly say grip; a selected radio button in an ungraded screenshot is not treated as a verified answer.
- Presentation 6 question 1 prints AN75-3 with 5/16 inch. The intended designation needs clarification; this item is excluded.
- Presentation 6 question 11 prints 5/15 inch for AN5-6A. Presentation 5 question 4 and the AN diameter rule establish 5/16 inch; the practice key corrects this typo explicitly.
- Presentation 7 question 25 calls AN392-6 a cotter pin and gives a 3/16-inch length. The existing course deck identifies AN392 as a clevis-pin family, and the designation is insufficiently clear to establish the intended dimension. This item is excluded pending correction.
- The new graded Presentation 7 review explicitly identifies its 82-degree image as AN505 and 100-degree image as AN507. These questions are scoped to those supplied illustrations; earlier conflicting slide angle statements remain flagged.
