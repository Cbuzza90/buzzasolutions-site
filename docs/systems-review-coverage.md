# Systems review coverage check

Checked against the 15-page **AMEM 10004 Review Test #1-1**, the earlier 50-question answer key, and all 29 newly supplied screenshots. Duplicate screenshots are treated as one source concept. The quizzes remain four-choice multiple choice with no numerical calculations.

## Result

- Test 1: 40 foundational questions; explanations clarified in the earlier update.
- Test 2: 83 applied questions, including 18 additions from this review check.
- Notes: all five laws/principles, fluid families and seals, properties, pressure/force/flow, layouts, six pressure-control components, reservoirs, stored energy, power packs, actuators and brakes.
- Grouped answers are expanded into individual questions: each of the five named laws, all six pressure-control roles, the mineral/vegetable/phosphate-ester seal associations, viscosity statements, reservoir functions, and both actuator types.
- Added original review images for mechanical advantage, open-centre neutral/operated states, a labelled closed-centre circuit and the reversible landing-gear power pack. The existing numbered-component, reservoir and fluid-comparison images remain.

## Source issues and deliberate limits

1. **Power-pack screenshot:** its sentence says the gear is being extended, while the figure shows “Up” arrows and gear-up components. The practice question tests reversible motor/pump flow and power-pack identification, not that inconsistent gear-state statement.
2. **Efficiency:** “almost 100%” is the review's introductory description, not a guarantee of measured efficiency in every installation. Notes and the question preserve that distinction.
3. **PDF page 7:** the worked force example rounds pressure early and reports 989.1 lb. For an ideal system using the stated radii, the unrounded area ratio gives 1,000 lb. Its line equating force to “small piston force × big piston area” also mislabels the pressure term. It is excluded from Systems practice.
4. **PDF page 6:** work divided by displacement gives the force along the direction of motion, not automatically aircraft weight. The example's wording conflates those quantities. It is excluded from Systems practice.
5. **Fluid identification:** colour and odour are study descriptions, not sufficient identification. Both 5606 and 83282 can be red. Fire-resistant does not mean fireproof; compatibility does not establish aircraft substitution approval.
6. **Gas-law wording:** the screenshot's matching dropdowns are closed, so their exact descriptions cannot be read. The new questions explicitly state constant temperature for Boyle and constant pressure for Charles. Conditions are essential to avoid overlapping answers.
7. **Aspirator:** review page 14 names the method and associates it with a variable-displacement pump, but does not show a detailed operating mechanism. Name recognition is covered; no unsupported mechanism diagram or question was added.
8. **Electro-hydraulic actuation:** page 15 includes an EHAS/F-35 overview graphic without a readable operating lesson or answer key. It is not used to invent detailed questions. If the instructor includes EHAS in Test 1, a dedicated source explanation would be useful.
