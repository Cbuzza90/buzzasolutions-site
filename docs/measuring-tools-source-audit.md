## Scope and source qualifications

The test covers tool identification/selection, layout tools, torque concepts and wrench types, care, every labelled micrometer part, direct imperial scale facts, calipers, depth tools and other inspection instruments. It does not introduce numerical calculations outside Math. Review Q2/Q3 conversions, Q9 extension calculations and Q10-Q14 measurement arithmetic are excluded, except Q14's explicit zero-reference instruction. Q6/Q7 scale increments are direct factual recall, not generated calculations.

- Slide 23 calls the tool a radius gauge but includes diameter captions. Questions and notes use radius/profile comparison and do not copy those captions.
- Slide 32's clockwise instruction is qualified as its right-hand-thread course example. It is not a universal direction restriction for all torque wrenches.
- Slide 57 states 0.0001-inch vernier increments but slide 58 illustrates a different scale. No blanket resolution question is generated. Notes direct the learner to the actual scale.
- Slide 70 groups infrared and laser beams together. The pictured pyrometer uses infrared sensing; the aiming laser does not measure temperature, as clarified by the manufacturer manual.
- Slide 25's desired-plus-drag relationship is tested as the supplied procedure, with a reminder to follow the applicable fastener instructions.
