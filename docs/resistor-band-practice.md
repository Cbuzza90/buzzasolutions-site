# Resistor band practice

Source: the user's Resistor Colour chart.ppt. The displayed `resistor-colour-chart-4-5.svg` clips the original course artwork to its four/five-band examples and digit/multiplier/tolerance table. The six-band illustration and temperature-coefficient column are excluded at the user's request. The original source remains archived, but is not linked from the viewer.

Each attempt has 20 unique questions: ten four-band and ten five-band resistors. Fields are resistance, tolerance, minimum and maximum, with all resistance quantities in ohms. No PPM questions, sixth bands or coefficient fields are displayed, generated or included in notes. Correct answers use the supplied chart and integer-scaled tolerance arithmetic.

Older six-band attempts become five-band attempts by removing the final band and obsolete answer field. Existing order, position, numeric answers, drafts and highest scores are retained. Migrated questions may share five-band colours because their former distinction was solely the deleted sixth band; this is accepted on restore to preserve progress. New generated attempts are unique.

On phones/tablets, the original-chart crop remains pinned with zoom/pan while a compact resistor drawing and two-column answer fields scroll independently below. Notes follow Check answers. Desktop retains its side-by-side chart. One-word recall still hides notes during attempts.

Validation covers source examples, blank cells, fractional multipliers, 20 unique questions even with constant random sources, numeric grading, unit migration, six-band save migration, persistence, best scores and searchable question references. Physical touch gestures and keyboard sizing still merit a real-device check.
