# Human Factors practice

Safety has two separate course buttons: Module 1 (Completed) and Human Factors Quiz. The latter opens `/study/safety/human-factors`, with its 32-question practice at `/study/safety/human-factors/practice-1`. Module 1 retains the three original mixed Safety tests.

The bank selects Human Factors questions from the existing supplied Safety review/answer-key banks. Each entry records its original bank number and question ID; wording, choices, explanations and correct answers are preserved. No additional lecture material or answer key is invented. This is a practice pool, not a claim to reproduce the upcoming 17-question classroom quiz.

Coverage includes every PEAR category, every Dirty Dozen factor, human-factors disciplines, error models, cause analysis and constructive responses. The 80% statistic remains explicitly attributed to the supplied introduction slides rather than presented as a universal figure. Fire, PPE and shop-safety questions are excluded.

The shared quiz engine shuffles questions/choices, saves the attempt and highest score independently of the three mixed Safety tests, and preserves the best score on reset. The original `/study/safety/module-1/practice-4` storage key is retained and its old URL redirects, preserving existing attempts. Human Factors has its own notes registration containing PEAR, Dirty Dozen, Error models, disciplines and searchable references for every question. Continue studying and the quiz's Tests navigation return to this separate section.
